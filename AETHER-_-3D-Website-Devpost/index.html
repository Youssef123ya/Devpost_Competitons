Æther

Every atom heavier than helium was forged inside a star. The iron in your blood was made in a supernova. Æther takes that fact and runs it backwards as a piece of visual music — one continuous cloud of 147,456 particles that never disappears and never reloads, only ever rearranges itself into the next thing it is about to become.

Scroll and the cloud passes through six states: formless drift, ignition, a spiral galaxy, a strand of DNA, a growing tree, and finally a word — matter organised well enough to name itself. Between each state the particles scatter into turbulence and reassemble, because nothing in the universe changes shape politely.

Everything you see is generated in the browser at runtime. There are no 3D models, no textures, no images — the six target shapes are written as mathematics, baked into a floating-point texture, and interpolated on the GPU. The soundtrack is synthesised live from six oscillators.

  • Three.js
  • WebGL2
  • GLSL
  • Custom vertex morphing
  • Float data textures
  • Custom bloom pipeline
  • ACES tone mapping
  • Web Audio API
  • Procedural geometry
  • No assets
